# Privacy Policy, outline (DRAFT, for legal review)

1. **Who we are.** The app's publisher (the adult account holder or LLC), with a contact email.
2. **The short version.** Scooter Bluetooth data stays on your phone. There are no ads and no data selling.
3. **Data we process on-device only.** BLE packets and logs, trip stats, optional GPS routes (only with explicit permission), themes, settings, and the garage. None of it is uploaded unless you export it or turn on sync.
4. **Data we collect if you create an account (optional).** Email or Sign in with Apple ID, entitlement status, and synced items you choose.
5. **Payments.** Apple handles App Store purchases. Stripe handles web purchases. We never see card numbers. We get the subscription status and a customer ID.
6. **Compatibility submissions (opt-in).** Exactly what's sent (anonymized GATT layout and sample frames), which you review before sending. Never credentials, location, serial numbers or MAC addresses.
7. **Crash reports.** Opt-in, with no personal data. Name the provider.
8. **Legal bases** (GDPR): contract (subscription), consent (sync, location, submissions, crash reports), legitimate interest (security).
9. **Retention and deletion.** In-app account deletion, and what's kept for legal or tax reasons.
10. **Your rights.** Access, deletion, correction and portability (GDPR). Know, delete, correct and opt out of sale/share (CCPA/CPRA; we don't sell or share).
11. **Children.** Not directed to under-13s. Describe the age gate if sync is offered.
12. **Processors.** Apple, Stripe, Supabase (or your host), and the crash-reporting provider.
